Model literature references
===========================

These references identify model origins and architectural foundations. Family
pages explain what FACETpy uses and changes. Experimental package names do not
establish reproduction of a paper or its results.

Use the publication year shown below. Where a preprint predates publication,
the linked arXiv record may show an earlier submission year.
